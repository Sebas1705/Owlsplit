# P14 · Member identity: leaving, returning and accounts — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use `mobiai-mobile-executing-plans-with-subagents` (recommended) or `mobiai-mobile-executing-plans` to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** What was decided in the [study](../designs/2026-10-12-member-identity.md) (option A): when leaving a group, nothing is lost and the member remembers whose it was.

- If you come back with the same account (anonymous or Google), you get your member back without choosing.
- If you come back with another account, you can recover your abandoned profile after confirming, and the group sees it in the history.
- The group sees which profiles are linked to Google.

**Architecture:**
- **Data.** The member gains `pastUids` (accounts that had it and left) and `linked` (account linked to Google).
  - Schema 2 → 3 (`2.sqm`, `3.db` snapshot), rows and DTO.
  - `Member.abandoned` = no account and has `pastUids`.
- **Leaving** (`GroupRepository.leave`). Together with `userUid → null`, it adds my uid to `pastUids` (without repeating it) and sets `linked = false` and `payMethods = null`.
- **Rules.**
  - `pastUids` is a list of at most 20. It only grows, and only with my own uid when I release my member. A new member is created without it.
  - `linked` is set only by the member's own person; it can be removed when releasing.
  - Tests in `subcollections.test.mjs`.
- **Domain.**
  - `MemberAccounts`:
    - `former` computes the accounts each member had: `pastUids` plus the history (`JOINED` entries and `userUid` changes), for groups older than P14.
    - `reclaimable` returns the member that this account left and nobody has claimed.
    - `rejoins` tells whether each return was with the same account or another one.
  - `ClaimMember`:
    - `reclaim` reassociates without asking and `formerAccounts` feeds the «¿Quién eres?» ("Who are you?") list.
    - On joining it publishes `linked` if the session is Google.
  - `SyncMyLinked` runs when Home opens and `LinkGoogle` publishes `linked` on linking.
- **UI.**
  - Join: if you are a former member, you go straight in. In «¿Quién eres?», abandoned profiles come first with «Salió del grupo · sus gastos siguen aquí» ("Left the group · their expenses are still here") (or «… era una cuenta de Google» ("… was a Google account")), and picking one asks «¿Eres Carlos?» ("Are you Carlos?").
  - History: «Carlos volvió al grupo» ("Carlos came back to the group") and «… con otra cuenta» ("… with another account").
  - Members: «Cuenta con Google» ("Google account"), «Con cuenta» ("Has an account"), «Salió del grupo» ("Left the group") or «Sin cuenta» ("No account").

**Depends on:** P13 and the flow fixes (#42).

---

## Decisions

1. **Option A** (owner, 2026-10-12). Recovering an abandoned profile with another account is open to anyone, but asks for confirmation and stays in the history, visible to everyone. No server or approvals needed.
2. **The history already kept the names.** Expenses and payments point to the member, not the account. What's new is that the member knows who had it, so it can be given back to them.
3. **Groups older than P14 aren't migrated by writing.** The rules only let you add your own uid to `pastUids`, so the app reads the history to recognize who left earlier.
4. **Google is the permanent identity.** Its uid is the same on any phone. With an anonymous account, you are recognized only as long as the app isn't reinstalled; option A covers the rest.
