# P11a · Languages and accessibility — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use `mobiai-mobile-executing-plans-with-subagents` (recommended) or `mobiai-mobile-executing-plans` to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Spanish and English from the MVP with a language picker in Profile ([3.10](../designs/2026-10-07-owlsplit-core-design.md#310-settings), [Localization](../designs/2026-10-07-owlsplit-core-design.md#localization)) and the spec's accessibility pass ([Accessibility](../designs/2026-10-07-owlsplit-core-design.md#accessibility)): AA contrast, text at 200 %, screen readers and touch targets.

**Architecture:**
- `core:datastore`: `DisplaySettings` (formerly `ThemeSettings`) with theme and language (`AppLanguage`: system, Spanish, English).
- `composeApp`: `LocalAppLocale` (expect/actual: on Android it overrides `Locale` and `LocalConfiguration`; on iOS `AppleLanguages`), `App` applies the language and the money format; it waits to read the preference so it doesn't show one language and then switch to the other.
- `core:ui`: `LocalMoneyFormat` and `moneyFormatFor(tag)` (Spanish style for `es*`, English for the rest); `dayLabel` with per-language templates ("12 jul" / "Jul 12"); `SwitchRow`; `OwlsplitTextField` with an accessible name and error.
- Resources: `values/` in English (default for any other device language) and `values-es/` in Spanish, across the nine modules.
- `core:testing` (androidMain): `assertAccessible()`, a semantics audit called by every screenshot test.

**Platform:** KMP (Android verified on emulator; iOS compiles in CI)

**Depends on:** P10.

---

## Decisions

1. **English by default**: a phone set to French or German sees the app in English; Spanish only with the device in Spanish or by choosing it. Language names in the picker are written in their own language ("Español", "English").
2. **Live switching**: choosing a language recomposes the app without restarting it or losing navigation; it is saved in DataStore. Text stored in data (names, descriptions) is not translated.
3. **Apostrophes**: Compose resources don't interpret `\'`; English uses the typographic apostrophe (’).
4. **Contrast**: `textMuted` becomes `#696D78` / `#80848F` and light `warning` becomes `#9A5B00`: all readable text meets AA (≥ 4.5:1) on background and surface in both themes.
5. **Text at 200 %**: screenshots with `RuntimeEnvironment.setFontScale(2f)`; from 150 % on, expense rows put the amounts below the description, and the link nudge gives the button the full free width.
6. **Audit**: Google's Accessibility Test Framework doesn't act under Robolectric (we checked with an unnamed 10 dp button that passed), so `assertAccessible()` walks the semantics tree: every clickable element needs a name and at least 40 dp visible (Compose expands the touch area to 48 dp, `minimumTouchTargetSize`). It found the unnamed switches and the unnamed empty text fields.
7. **Reduce motion**: the only animation (the counting balance) respects the system animation scale on Android; on iOS it is solved in P11c.
