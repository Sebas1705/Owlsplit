Owlsplit 1.0 is here: the first stable version.
• Groups and expenses in several currencies, recurring ones too.
• Settle up with Bizum, PayPal, Revolut or a SEPA QR.
• Works offline and syncs for the whole group.
• Guests can join from the browser, no app needed.
• New: "Where does this come from?" explains each balance step by step.
• Stats, a widget, CSV/PDF export and Splitwise import.
• Link Google to keep your groups when you change phones.
Thanks to everyone who tested the betas!
