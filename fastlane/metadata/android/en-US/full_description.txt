Owlsplit keeps your group's accounts so nobody has to: trips, flatshares, dinners, gifts or anything else.

<b>Add an expense in seconds</b>
• Who paid (several people too), how much and who shares it.
• Split equally, by amounts, by percentages or by shares (a couple can count double).
• Several currencies in one group, with ECB exchange rates.
• Repeating expenses add themselves: rent, bills, subscriptions.

<b>Settle up in one tap</b>
• Owlsplit works out the fewest payments to get everyone even.
• Open PayPal or Revolut with the amount filled in, or copy the Bizum details in one tap.
• Every payment asks for confirmation and can be undone.

<b>Everyone sees the same</b>
• Invite with a link or a QR code; no sign-up needed.
• Works offline and syncs when you're back.
• A history of every change: who, what and when, and you can restore what was deleted.
• If someone leaves and comes back, they get their profile and expenses back.

<b>Your data stays yours</b>
• Group summary and CSV and PDF export.
• Import your groups from Splitwise.
• Link your account to Google so you lose nothing when you change phones.
• No ads, and open source (MIT licence).
